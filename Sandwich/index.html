SANDWHICH

(serves-1)

Hello sandwhich lovers!!!
I'll be teaching you how to make the tastiest sandwhich you've ever had in your life. And I mean it. It's gonna be healty and tasty also won't take up much of your time.

Let's get started and I hope you'll enjoy it.

Ingredients

  • 4- Breads
  • 1 medium- Boiled potato
  • 100g- Cottage cheese
  • Spices (oregano, salt, red chilli flakes)
  • 1- Sliced onion
  • 1- Sliced tomato
  • Ketchup
  • Mayo
  • 50g- Butter

Instructions

  1. Mash the boiled potatoes and add spices to it.
  2. Roast all four bread slices
  3. Apply mashed potato on two bread slices
  4. Shallow fry cottage cheese and sliced tomato and onion in a pan with some spices
  5. Now put these on top of mashed potato.
  6. Mix 2 spoon mayo with one spoon of ketchup.
  7. Apply mayo-ketchup mixture on the other two slice of breads.
  8. Sprinkle seasonings on all the bread slices.
  9. Now put the mayo-ketchup mixture bread slice on top of the mashed potato one(repeate it for other pair of bread slices as well).
  10. Cut them in half, either diagonally or however it pleases you.
